Q2

实际上很简单，找到排名前十的全垒打选手即可，要求是这些选手必须在PA州的学校打过，并且成绩从高到低排序，同分则按first name排序。 涉及到的表有四个：schools、collegeplaying、appearances、people。

1with player_year as (select playerID, yearID from schools as s join collegeplaying as c on s.schoolID = c.schoolID where s.state = 'PA'),
2player_hr as (select * from appearances join player_year on appearances.playerID = player_year.playerID),
3player_hr_top10 as (select playerID, MAX(HR) as max_hr_appearance from player_hr group by playerID order by max_hr_appearance desc limit 10)
4
5select people.nameFirst || ' (' || people.nameGiven || ') ' || people.nameLast as name, player_hr_top10.max_hr_appearance from player_hr_top10 join people on player_hr_top10.playerID = people.playerID order by player_hr_top10.max_hr_appearance desc, people.nameFirst asc;

遵循的步骤也很简单：

  1. 先找到在PA州学校干过的选手
  2. 再从appperances表中确认匹配的选手和全垒打次数
  3. 针对选手来group一下，找到一个选手的最高记录，并且找到前十
  4. 最终对最高记录进行排序，并且从people表中拼凑完整名称，并且按照要求输出

Q3

题目要求找到在1999年之后（> 1999）赢得Gold Golve奖的选手，并且击球比他所在队伍自从1999年（>= 1999）的平均次数还要多。

1with player_gold as (select * from awardsplayers as a where a.awardID = 'Gold Glove' and a.yearID > 1999),
2player_team as (select a.yearID, a.playerID, a.teamID, a.lgID, a.G_batting from appearances as a join player_gold as p on p.playerID=a.playerID and p.yearID=a.yearID where a.yearID >= 1999),
3player_team_grouped as (select p.playerID, p.teamID, p.G_batting, p.yearID from player_team as p join leagues as l on p.lgID = l.lgID where l.active = 'Y' group by p.playerID, p.teamID, p.G_batting, p.yearID),
4player_outstanding as (select * from player_team_grouped as p where p.G_batting > (select AVG(a.G_batting) from appearances as a where a.teamID == p.teamID and a.yearID >= 1999))
5
6select p.nameGiven, o.teamID, COUNT(DISTINCT o.yearID) as distinct_years from player_outstanding as o join people as p on o.playerID = p.playerID group by p.nameGiven, o.teamID order by distinct_years desc, p.nameGiven asc limit 10;
  1. 找到1999年获得对应奖项的选手
  2. 筛选对应的队伍、年份、击球次数
  3. 分组，并且筛选联盟仍在active
  4. 再筛选一遍，要求比队友击球次数多，这里用到了相关子查询，感觉就是join的简便写法
  5. 组合出符合要求的输出

Q4

要求筛选出所有队伍：一年如果有5个不同选手获奖，并且有经理获奖，看起来要求很简单，但分散的表实在太多，得写很多子查询。

 1with active_teams as (select t.yearID, t.teamID from teams as t join leagues as l on t.lgID=l.lgID where l.active = 'Y'),
 2player_team as (select active_teams.teamID, active_teams.yearID, a.playerID, a.lgID from active_teams join appearances as a on active_teams.yearID = a.yearID and active_teams.teamID = a.teamID),
 3player_award as (select p.yearID, p.teamID, p.playerID, a.awardID from player_team as p join awardsplayers as a on p.playerID = a.playerID and p.yearID = a.yearID and p.lgID = a.lgID),
 4manager_team as (select a.yearID, a.teamID, m.playerID, m.lgID from active_teams as a join managers as m on m.yearID = a.yearID and m.teamID = a.teamID),
 5manager_award as (select m.yearID, m.teamID, m.playerID, a.awardID from manager_team as m join awardsmanagers as a on m.playerID = a.playerID and a.yearID = m.yearID and m.lgID = a.lgID),
 6player_award_5 as (select p.yearID, p.teamID, COUNT(distinct p.playerID) as counts from player_award as p group by p.yearID, p.teamID having COUNT(distinct p.playerID) > 5),
 7manager_award_1 as (select m.yearID, m.teamID from manager_award as m group by m.yearID, m.teamID having COUNT(distinct m.playerID) >= 1),
 8team_year as (select p.teamID, p.yearID from player_award_5 as p join manager_award_1 as m on p.yearID = m.yearID and p.teamID = m.teamID),
 9team_count as (select teams.lgID as league, teams.name as team_name, COUNT(distinct team_year.yearID) as distinct_years from team_year join teams on team_year.teamID = teams.teamID and team_year.yearID = teams.yearID group by teams.lgID, teams.name HAVING COUNT(distinct team_year.yearID) > 1)
10
11select l.league, t.team_name, t.distinct_years from team_count as t join leagues as l on l.lgID = t.league order by t.distinct_years DESC, t.team_name ASC;
  1. 找到在活跃联盟的队伍
  2. 对于这些队伍，找到匹配的选手
  3. 筛选这些选手中的获奖选手
  4. 对于这些队伍，找到匹配的经理
  5. 找到这些经理中的获奖经理
  6. 找到这些队伍中，同一年获奖超过5个不同选手的队伍
  7. 找到这些队伍中，同一年获奖超过1个经理的队伍
  8. 找到6、7两步的交集，并且统计队伍满足条件的年份数量
  9. 取联盟名称，并设置题目要求输出，注意排序

这道题很变态，其实要的东西不难，就是分散在各个表里面，实在是非常绕，当然上面肯定有可以简化的地方，但这里不强求了。

Q5

要求找到名人堂的最早队友，若有多个，则按名称排序，最终筛选出前10的名人堂队友。

 1with hof_player as (select distinct h.playerID from halloffame as h where h.inducted = 'Y'),
 2hof_player_team as (select h.playerID, a.yearID, a.teamID from hof_player as h join appearances as a on h.playerID = a.playerID),
 3hof_player_name as (select h.playerID, h.yearID, h.teamID, p.nameFirst || ' (' || p.nameGiven || ') ' || p.nameLast as name from hof_player_team as h join people as p on h.playerID = p.playerID),
 4appearances_name as (select a.yearID, a.teamID, a.playerID, p.nameFirst || ' (' || p.nameGiven || ') ' || p.nameLast as name from appearances as a join people as p on a.playerID = p.playerID),
 5all_name as (select h.name as hof_player_name, earliest_teammate_name, earliest_teammate_year from hof_player_name as h cross join (
 6    select a.name as earliest_teammate_name, a.yearID as earliest_teammate_year from appearances_name as a
 7    where h.yearID = a.yearID and h.teamID = a.teamID and h.playerID != a.playerID
 8    order by a.name asc limit 1
 9)),
10ranked as (select *, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (partition by hof_player_name order by earliest_teammate_year asc, earliest_teammate_name asc) as rank from all_name)
11
12select hof_player_name, earliest_teammate_name, earliest_teammate_year from ranked where ranked.rank = 1 order by hof_player_name asc limit 10;
  1. 找到名人堂入选者，并且去重
  2. 找到名人堂入选者加入的队伍以及年份
  3. 再构造一个名称字段
  4. 针对所有选手构造名称字段（感觉和第三步有点重复，写的时候居然没发现）
  5. 使用cross join筛选最早队友，按名称排序，只取一个

Q6

这道题要求对teams表重新插入24年的所有队伍信息，把franchID作为teamID更新一把，如果失败了，就把attendence设置为-1。 很少操作insert，所以直接问AI求了一波答案，所以风格更简洁：

 1INSERT INTO teams (yearID, lgID, teamID, franchID, divID, teamRank, name, attendance)
 2SELECT
 3    yearID,
 4    lgID,
 5    franchID AS teamID,
 6    NULL AS franchID,
 7    divID,
 8    teamRank,
 9    name,
10    attendance
11FROM teams
12WHERE yearID = 2024
13ON CONFLICT (yearID, lgID, teamID) DO UPDATE
14SET attendance = -1;

这里要注意，新记录的franchID要设置为空，题目的隐藏条件。
